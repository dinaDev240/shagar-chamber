ABOUT SHAGAR CHAMBER

Building a Stronger Business Community

Shagar Chamber of Commerce & Sectoral Association works to connect businesses, entrepreneurs and institutions with opportunities that support business development and economic growth.

The Chamber provides a platform for businesses across different sectors to connect, exchange knowledge, discover markets, develop partnerships and access services that can strengthen their operations.

Through business networking, information sharing, training, market connections, advocacy and collaboration, the Chamber seeks to contribute to a more productive and connected business environment.
